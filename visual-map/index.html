    Extend the graphical interface¶

    Open Knowledge Architecture.canvas from this folder in Obsidian. Drag notes from the file explorer into the Canvas; resize and group cards; label connections with their relation type. Add your own diagrams or images to this folder and embed them in notes. Describe what each visual asserts and its source. Canvas edges do not automatically update the typed relations in Markdown: reconcile them during review.

    The SVG is a separate editable overview, not a live projection of the Canvas. Published readers can use this note's map and links without depending on a Canvas renderer. Canvas rendering on the target Publish site remains untested.
